# Public portable edition

Original portable release: 2026-11-03. GitHub packaging update: 2026-11-10. Author-walk procedure, unit roles and the marker script added: 2026-11-23.

This edition adapts a writing procedure developed through repeated academic drafting and revision. It retains the construction order, purpose-specific paragraph guidance, source/editorial review, unprimed reader check, author walk, delivery discipline and learning procedure.

It deliberately excludes private research examples, draft history, conversations, personal preferences tied to named people, local file locations and agent-platform operations. The examples here are newly constructed teaching examples. They do not establish scientific results or prescribe a paper's model.

Model-specific roles have been replaced by functions: editorial lead, canonical writer, source/editorial reviewer, fresh reader and qualified scientific reviewer. These may be humans or appropriately capable assistants. No brand receives automatic authority over evidence or prose quality. The host project's scientific authorship and verification requirements remain binding.

## The author walk

The author-walk procedure generalizes what one author's corrections taught two writers during a long revision. The corrections, the manuscript and the accepted and rejected drafts are not included; the unit-role examples are invented. Its evidence section reports one blind replay on a single paper with model judges. That is weak evidence, stated so that users measure their own first-pass acceptance instead of assuming it.

## Exemplar use

One useful bibliographic starting point in economics is Duffie, D., Gârleanu, N., and Pedersen, L. H. (2005), “Over-the-Counter Markets,” *Econometrica*, 73(6), 1815–1847. It is a suggested source to obtain and read for a matching task, not an included text, a universal section template, or a claim that the current reader has inspected it. Use other published exemplars when the genre, result or audience requires them.

## Rhetorical examples

The grouped cues about manufactured drama, forced symmetry, decorative explanation and repeated navigation were informed by an author-supplied [scholarly-revision package](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LWhlrN4um6POdyHJ3QYER_s2bNVMYORm/view). This edition paraphrases a small set of general editorial ideas and supplies its own examples; it does not redistribute that package or its scripts. The external link is attribution, not a runtime dependency. A rhetorical cue is not evidence of AI authorship.

## Limits

The procedure is intended to improve preparation and reduce avoidable corrections. It does not guarantee a first-pass acceptance percentage, prove scientific claims, establish publication readiness by a checklist score, or ensure that every agent host loads instructions correctly. Record actual use, rework and author waiting before claiming improvement.

The optional helper records versions and prepares review packets. It cannot judge truth, choose the paper's contribution, infer every dependency, verify reviewer independence, or approve delivery. Its manifests and packets can contain local paths and private text; they belong in the working project and should not be published automatically.
