# Public portable edition

History: original portable release 2026-11; packaging update 2026-11; walk procedure, unit roles and the marker script added 2026-11; rebuilt 2026-11.

This edition adapts a writing procedure developed through repeated academic drafting and revision with one author. It keeps the structure and the procedure: two writers, a fresh reader, a plan before paragraphs, a card for each paragraph, a send check, up to three versions per part, a project record, and a way to learn from corrections.

## What the tree contains

- The entry: `SKILL.md`. The team file: `HOW_WE_WORK.md`. The standard: `STANDARDS.md`. How a correction enters the skill: `MAINTENANCE.md`.
- `references/`: the unit file (plan, card, send check), the walk, the runbook for a prepared paper section, unit roles, build checks, claim fidelity, the grant package.
- `types/`: one sheet per type of text.
- `templates/`: forms for the plan message, the part message and the project record.
- `examples/published.md`: short cited quotes from published articles.
- `scripts/card_check.py`: a marker script.

The 2026-11 rebuild removed the files of the earlier edition: the handbook, the review protocol, the helper guide and its script, the older reference notes, and the sample input files. Nothing in the tree links to them.
`references/unit-roles.md` was rewritten from scratch in the same rebuild, with new examples.

## What it promises

- No private material. It contains no manuscripts, draft history, conversations, quotations of the author, names of people or projects, local file locations or platform operations.
- Invented examples. Every teaching example is newly constructed, in settings unrelated to the research the procedure grew from (a clinic's appointment system, a record of water-pump repairs, flood warnings between towns). None establishes a scientific result or prescribes a paper's model.
- Seats by function. The seats are lead, partner writer, fresh reader, fidelity reader, helper, coordinator (for a grant application), mathematics author and outside reviewer. Any may be a person or a capable assistant. No brand receives automatic authority over evidence or prose.
- Principles, not rulings. Where the private procedure rests on the author's words, this edition states the principle plainly instead.
- The host project's scientific authorship and verification rules stay binding. The skill grants no authority to change science, publish or send anything.

## Exemplar use

[Published examples](examples/published.md) holds short quotes from published journal articles, each cited with its page, sorted by the job the passage does. It does not include the articles. Obtain them lawfully to read further. Use other exemplars when the genre, result or audience requires them.

## Rhetorical cues

Some cues in the standard (against manufactured drama, template scaffolds, decorative explanation and repeated navigation) were informed by an author-supplied scholarly-revision package. That package is not redistributed or linked here. This edition paraphrases general editorial ideas and supplies its own examples. A rhetorical cue is not evidence of who or what wrote a text.

## Evidence and limits

The evidence is small. [README.md](README.md) states it.
- One blind replay on one paper: nine passages redrafted with the walk procedure and compared with the author's approved text.
- One before-and-after comparison of the procedure itself: fresh readers traced the same twelve writing jobs through the old instructions and through the rebuilt ones, and counted contradictions and points left to guess. It measures how clear the procedure is. It does not measure the quality of prose.

The procedure aims to reduce avoidable corrections and author waiting. It does not guarantee a first-pass acceptance rate, prove claims, or certify a text by checklist. `scripts/card_check.py` finds markers and certifies nothing. Record actual use and rework before claiming improvement.
A project record holds private feedback and manuscript text. Keep it in the working project; do not publish it.
