# Independent review of the actual passage

Review the identified candidate and its joins. Distinguish scientific fidelity, effective information selection and what a reader actually understands. No PASS label covers all three by itself.

## Three complementary lenses

| Lens | Inputs | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Source and editorial | Candidate, preceding context, sources, design, current author choices and protected material | Located findings on truth, specific attribution, scope, completeness, selection, order and job fit |
| Fresh reader | Publication-facing candidate, necessary preceding prose and audience only | Own reconstruction, expected next step and consequential confusion before seeing intended answers |
| Architecture and published-exemplar comparison | Complete candidate unit, relevant sources, actual matched published units and author constraints | Consequential construction differences, whether they transfer, repairs or justified differences |

One qualified independent reviewer can cover source/editorial and architecture. A fresh reader must have an unprimed context; renaming a fully informed seat does not make it fresh. Models may assist, but model brand and confidence are not evidence of correctness.

For initial drafts or major reconstructions, use a substantive independent assembled-section or paper assessment rather than a separate external consultation for every paragraph. External consultation still requires applicable authorization. Reuse still-valid judgments for local changes.

## Construct the packets carefully

The source/editorial reviewer should see enough evidence to challenge both the draft and its design. Do not ask only whether each sentence is literally true; ask whether its placement and implied causal link fit the author's task.

The fresh reader must not see design notes, expected answers, internal comments, approval history, preferred replacement text or previous verdicts. Use publication-facing text or actual rendered figures, with the definitions a real reader would have encountered. Raw manuscript source can leak comments. If context is insufficient, repair the packet before blaming the prose.

Start with open questions: What do you understand? What question is answered? What would you expect next? Where did you pause in a way that changes your interpretation? Specific inferential probes can follow; do not lead the reader to the desired answer.

## Reconcile findings

A useful finding identifies location, consequence, evidence and the smallest adequate repair. Different taste alone is not a defect. “No material defect; retain this passage” is valid.

The lead compares the reader's reconstruction with supported meaning, while separately judging relevance. A reader can understand an unnecessary paragraph. An editor can prefer a stronger architecture even when the old wording is comprehensible. Preserve both observations.

Resolve disagreements explicitly. An applicable author choice governs supported presentation preferences. It does not make a scientific contradiction true. Conversely, a reviewer demanding every stronger available theorem may be imposing an irrelevant completeness standard. Check the actual promised result and current scope.

When a source check removes an overclaim, preserve the passage's legitimate purpose in a supported form in the same pass. Repair the earliest failed layer and recheck affected consumers. Do not send raw reviews to the author as a substitute for a coherent draft.

## Scientific verification is separate

New or materially changed proof work requires two fresh independent qualified reviewers before an established/proved label: an adversarial proof review and an independent logic review with checks of checkable objects. Neither is the author certifying its own derivation. Follow any stronger project-specific requirement. Computational agreement is not a proof of untested logical steps.

Unchanged reviewed mathematics need not be re-derived because prose moves. A seemingly small word change can alter quantifiers or scope and must be assessed accordingly. Editorial acceptance and author approval never upgrade scientific status.

## Stop proportionately

Stop when material findings are resolved, affected joins are sound, required reviews are complete and the artifact serves its intended reader. Additional review needs a changed input, unresolved material finding, unreliable evidence or explicit requirement. There is no default objection quota, unanimous-taste requirement or fixed count of clean rounds.

A budget ending does not convert an unresolved finding to PASS. Record the remaining issue, owner and next action. Independent useful work can continue without holding the author's next unrelated decision.
