# Model setup: teach the game before its solution

The reader should be able to reconstruct the model's actors, feasible actions, information, timing, state changes, payoffs and equilibrium concept. Formal accuracy and readable organization are both required. Do not let the source document's proof order determine the setup's reading order.

## Design the setup

Recover the adopted model, actual source version, reviewed results, current decisions and adjacent prose. Identify what the first result requires the reader to know. Preserve scientific assumptions, off-path rules that affect deviations, corners and commitment technology.

Map reader questions before drafting:

1. Who acts, with what endowment or resource, and which choices are feasible?
2. What state describes the economic situation, and what changes it?
3. What does each actor observe, and when?
4. When is an instruction sent, received, valid, cancelled and executed?
5. Which payments or capital changes occur at which event?
6. What is each objective and comparison baseline?
7. Which objects constitute equilibrium, and which consistency conditions determine them?
8. What maintained economic regime is studied, with what restrictions?

This is a coverage map, not a mandatory sequence of eight subsections. Group connected questions so the reader can follow the actual mechanism. Introduce an agent's endowment and feasible actions together, but do not collect every sentence mentioning that agent in one block. Equilibrium consistency belongs after choices and payoffs; income conventions belong with payoff accounting.

## State rules plainly

Use compact notation when it clarifies a distribution, state transition or payoff. Gloss symbols with the economic object they represent. Avoid replacing a short defined symbol with an unwieldy verbal expression simply to reduce notation.

Keep one name per concept. A term change must reach captions, proofs and later recalls. Submission, delivery and execution are distinct events when the model makes them distinct. State the event that changes the state or transfers value.

Before deleting a detail as redundant, identify the retained rule that implies it. Check information uniquely carried by prose, not just symbol definitions and references. An unreferenced sentence may still specify a necessary action restriction or off-path continuation.

An operative rule may live in a main-text footnote where the paper remains self-contained. It need not be duplicated in the body. Do not move it solely to a proof appendix because the main paragraph looks cleaner.

## Separate assumptions, choices and results

A restriction on available actions is different from a proved incentive not to take an available action. Rewriting “cannot cancel” as “does not want to cancel” changes the model claim and needs a specified cancellation technology and reviewed argument.

A selected continuation is not a unique equilibrium. A strategy verified later need not be imposed in the equilibrium definition. A comparative-static result should not be used to define an institution unless that is explicitly the adopted model.

For a substantive modeling choice, explain its economic role concisely where needed: what it captures, isolates or holds fixed. Do not claim robustness merely to defend a simplification. An assumption gloss should describe the intended regime without asserting a stronger consequence than the joint hypotheses establish.

An institutional analogy needs a checked mapping: trigger, information, action, delay and execution condition. Two true sentences can imply a false equivalence when juxtaposed. Attribute the specific institutional fact to its source; do not let that citation appear to validate the entire model.

## Equilibrium definition

Name the equilibrium objects first, then state best responses and market consistency using already defined choices and objectives. Preserve the deviation class, information restrictions, pure versus mixed behavior and quantities held fixed in a unilateral deviation.

Keep the definition concise. Do not explain every ordinary term or add solved behavior to make it sound helpful. Necessary counting conventions and exclusions can be attached to the whole definition in a footnote.

If a prescribed strategy is removed from the definition, check downstream quantifiers. Constructing one symmetric continuation does not classify all symmetric continuations. The scope of later uniqueness, multiplicity and comparative-static claims must remain clear.

## Review and application

The source/editorial reviewer sees the design and joined setup/first-result text. The fresh reader sees the actual prose and enough context to reconstruct the game. Ask what actions are available, who observes what, which event changes the state and what equilibrium requires. A material false inference requires repair; a taste difference does not.

Route objections to the right layer: wording, teaching step, density, placement, scientific scope or changed preference. Repeated confusion may require a new section map rather than another synonym. Preserve good wording when moving it. Recheck affected consumers and retire obsolete markup after authorized application.
