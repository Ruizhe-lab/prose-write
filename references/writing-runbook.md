# Preparing a paper section without the author present

Paths are relative to the skill folder.
"The team file" is [`HOW_WE_WORK.md`](../HOW_WE_WORK.md). "The unit file" is [`unit-plan-card-gate.md`](unit-plan-card-gate.md).
They hold the mode, the start of a job, the plan, the two writers, the versions, the send check, the fresh reader, the reading copy, the ready notice, approval, delivery, the record and the report.
This file holds only what preparing a whole paper section adds.

## When this applies

- A paper section of more than one part, new, rebuilt or revised, from reviewed research, in prepared mode.
- Which mode you are in: the team file, "Live or prepared". The steps of prepared work: the team file, "Prepared work".
- New, rebuild or revision, and what each owes: the team file, "The outside review".
- Not this file: a live walk ([`author-walk.md`](author-walk.md)); a grant ([`types/grant.md`](../types/grant.md)); one paragraph (the unit file).
- With this file: the type sheet in `types/` and [`examples/published.md`](../examples/published.md). After applying: [`paper-build-checks.md`](paper-build-checks.md).

## Principles

### CLOSE-TO-FINISHED  The author walks only a finished, assembled section
Principle: the author's time goes to near-finished work, executed to a high standard. Preparation, checking and repair happen before.

### SEVERAL-JUDGES  An assembled section is judged through separate lenses
Principle: no single reviewer sees everything. Several judges, each with its own lens and its own inputs, read the assembled section.
- Lens 1 is the fresh read of the whole. Lens 2 is the fidelity read of the whole. Who runs each: the unit file.
- Lens 3 is the lead's own judgment of economics and taste.

## Workflow, step by step

These steps sit inside "Prepared work" of the team file. The first four come before the plan message. The last three come after every part has passed the send check, and before the ready notice.

1. **Read the sources.** The manuscript at the job's commit. The reviewed sources: propositions, tables, the source set the record names. The author's accepted passages, where the project keeps them.
2. **Sort the content** into four lists, written below the plan in `plan_<section>.md`:
   - What must stay true: model rules, equilibrium class, estimand, quantifiers, domains, units, proof status, evidence limits.
   - What the author chose: contribution, emphasis, coauthor text kept.
   - What the writers decide: order, sentences, notation consistency, placement.
   - What is open.
3. **The paper's promise.** Write five lines in the same file, never in the paper: question, supported answer, mechanism or identification, closest comparison, consequence. Tie each central result to the line it serves. For an existing paper, start from its best argument.
4. **Section duties.** Write three lines: the question this section receives, the answer it gives, the objects the next section uses. Check both joins. For each prerequisite ask: is it needed to read the statement, needed for its economics, or only for the proof? Only for the proof: it goes to the proof.
5. **Assemble.** Read the whole section once, in order, without comments. Check each promise one section makes to another. A repetition to remove is listed as [`author-walk.md`](author-walk.md), "Assemble and sign off", says.
6. **The lenses** (below), on the assembled section.
7. **New or rebuilt section only:** the published comparison (below), and the outside review (the team file).

Two rules for drafting before the author has agreed the plan.
- Each queue sheet names the plan line it rests on.
- A decision changes a plan line: mark every sheet that rests on it "stale" and redraft those parts. Keep the others.

A scientific gap is not fixed by wording. Name it at its real scope. Route it to the project's proof and review gates.

## The lenses

Run lenses 1 and 2 at the same time, on the same commit. Keep their inputs apart.

| Lens | Gets | Never gets | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Fresh read | What the unit file gives a fresh reader, for the whole section | Plan, cards, intended answer | The unit file's pass, against the plan's point |
| 2. Fidelity read | What the unit file gives the fidelity reader, and [`theory-claim-fidelity.md`](theory-claim-fidelity.md) | Nothing withheld | The unit file's pass for the fidelity read |
| 3. Economics and taste | Text, plan, the matched passages of [`examples/published.md`](../examples/published.md) | Nothing withheld | Every formal object has its economic meaning; interpretation is sized to the result; placement matches the example, or the difference is justified in writing |

- A finding names: place, consequence for the reader or the science, reason, smallest repair. "No defect; keep it" is valid.
- Lenses disagree: write both findings side by side in the sheet. Never average them. The lead decides and says why.
- A scientific contradiction stays open until its source settles it.
- When the loop ends: the unit file (CHECK-ON-ARRIVAL).

## Published comparison (new section or rebuild)

- Who: the lead.
- How: open the role in [`examples/published.md`](../examples/published.md) that does the same job. Read the setup before it and the explanation after it. Write below the plan one or two construction choices and whether each transfers.
- Pass: each consequential gap is repaired, or the difference is justified by the reader's job or the science.
- Counts and sentence metrics decide nothing. They are information only.

## Checks before the author sees it

These rows add to the checks of a prepared whole (the unit file).

| Check | Who | How | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sources sorted | Lead | Opens `plan_<section>.md` | The four lists, the five promise lines and the three duty lines are written |
| Joins | Lead | Reads the last paragraph of the section before and the first of the section after | The question received and the objects handed on match the duty lines |
| Lens 1, fresh read of the whole | The unit file says who | Lens 1 row | Pass written in the record |
| Lens 2, fidelity read of the whole | The unit file says who | Lens 2 row | No material finding open |
| Lens 3, economics and taste | Lead | Lens 3 row | Each finding repaired, or kept with a written reason |
| Published comparison (new or rebuilt only) | Lead | The section above | Each gap repaired or justified, in writing |
| Outside review | Lead | The team file, "The outside review", only where it applies | The outside reviewer's written report exists; findings handled, or "outside review pending" stated |
| Build | Lead or helper | [`paper-build-checks.md`](paper-build-checks.md), the checks it names for a prepared reading copy | Every check passes |

## Limits

- The split into three lenses is the maintainers' design, not a tested rule.
- This file adds nothing to the fidelity read. Who runs it, what it gets and what passes it: the unit file.
- The pass line of lens 3 is a judgment, written by the writers.

## Craft notes (trial)

- Draft the central results and their explanations first; the opening, abstract and conclusion after.
- Lens 3 writes what the section must achieve before reading the plan, so the plan does not anchor it.
