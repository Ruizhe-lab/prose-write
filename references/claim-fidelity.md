# Claim fidelity

Map each changed sentence to its controlling source. A defensible source quotation does not automatically support the sentence's implied comparison or mechanism.

## Common dimensions

Check actor, object, population, denominator, units, conditioning, timing, horizon, expected versus realized quantity, comparison, held-fixed quantities, adjustment and claim strength. Distinguish levels, shares, growth rates, stocks, flows, event duration and occupancy fractions.

Keep a source trace to the actual theorem, proof step, definition, dataset cell or calculation. For numerical or magnitude claims, inspect the source value, units, sample, rounding and uncertainty. A strict sign supports direction; it does not establish a “large” effect. A decomposition must add to its headline within justified rounding or be corrected.

## Theory

- Which object is unique: an action given parameters, a mode-specific solution, a selected equilibrium, or the full game's equilibrium?
- Is the statement pointwise, expected, long-run average or optimized?
- Does a fixed-parameter deviation comparison become an equilibrium comparative static only after endogenous quantities adjust?
- Does “chooses” mean optimization, “is determined by” mean a closure, and “is fixed” mean exogenous?
- Are feasibility, information, commitment, entry and corner conditions retained?
- Is an action prohibited, available but suboptimal, or imposed by a selection convention? Those are different scientific claims.
- Does a parameter limit represent the described case under the actual timing? An unavailable timing state is not automatically a limit.
- “Closed form” means an explicit formula; being the root of a single equation is a different claim.
- Do adjustment, entry and survival verbs describe actual dynamics in the model, or merely comparisons of static equilibria?
- Does a common sufficient domain govern every conclusion that relies on it? A wider unused domain can remain technical support.

Check explanations as carefully as statements. If income rises because saved losses exceed foregone revenue, do not replace that attribution with “higher revenue outweighs the cost” unless the proof establishes that channel. An expected accounting identity is not a rule assigning each realized payment.

## Empirics

Keep the estimand, sample, admission/exclusion rules, outcome units, horizon, comparison, identification assumptions and uncertainty. Correlation is not causation. Evidence consistent with a mechanism need not distinguish it from alternatives. A finite-horizon effect is not permanent merely because it persists through the observed window.

Separate aggregate quantities from per-unit returns and population averages from within-unit changes. Compositional changes can move an average without changing each member's outcome. A changed sample can invalidate a previously accurate descriptive sentence even when its words stay the same.

Search the relevant exhibits and definitions before declaring a contradiction. Different populations, horizons or denominators may explain apparent disagreement; establish that explanation rather than assuming it.

## Repair and propagation

State the exact supported object in clear prose, retaining necessary scope without copying the audit's scaffolding into the paper. When a claim, term, label or definition changes, check its consumers in introduction, results, captions, empirical predictions, conclusion and appendix. Keep scientific status visible in working records. If the nearest supported replacement would abandon the author's purpose, expose the genuine choice instead of silently changing the paper.
