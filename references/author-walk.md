# The live walk: what it adds

Read after [`HOW_WE_WORK.md`](../HOW_WE_WORK.md) ("the team file") and [`unit-plan-card-gate.md`](unit-plan-card-gate.md) ("the unit file").
They hold the start of a job, the plan, the versions, the send check, approval, delivery and the record.
This file holds only what a walk adds. A walk: the author goes through a text with the writers, part by part.
Status: trial. The steps were written by the writers.
Evidence: one blind test on nine passages of one paper: closer to the author's approved text in seven of nine. Measure your own.

## The scope message

Send it only when the author works live on a text of several sections whose depth is not named. One message, before any plan. Prepared work has no scope message: the first message holds the plans and states the depth assumed per section.
1. Read the whole text, and the author's earlier texts of the same type.
2. List every section by its heading in the version the author reads.
3. For each section say who wrote it and the proposed depth. The owner decides the content; unclear: "apparent owner", to confirm. If the open problems are research decisions prose cannot fix, ask which comes first: a decisions sitting or the walk.
   - Full walk: the author's own sections, and those that carry the point.
   - Material only: a coauthor's section, or one the author calls fine.
   - Cut plan: only when asked.
4. End with the delivery question, when the text sits in a shared repository (the team file, "Approval and applying").
5. Write the answers word for word in `DECISIONS.md`. A declined class of change is not proposed again.

Pass: the author has confirmed each section's depth.

## The queue

- One sheet per part, on [`templates/QUEUE_SHEET.md`](../templates/QUEUE_SHEET.md), in the record's `queue/` folder.
- Before showing a sheet, replace its current text with the text as applied. An older proposal is never the current text.

## Assemble and sign off

1. When the last part is applied, the lead reads the section whole, once.
2. A new or rebuilt section: start its outside review now (the team file, "The outside review"). Do not wait for it.
3. A repetition to remove: list each passage in full, where it was, which part already says it, and why.
4. Send one message with that list and the coauthor note (Close, 1). Its last line is the sign-off question.
5. No answer: the text stays pending and is listed as open.
6. After a yes: remove the pending marks, deliver, prove delivery. Then the section report.

## A coauthor's section, or a part the author calls fine

- Who does what: the team file, "Who writes, by type of text" and "One message".
- Steps and checks: [`types/paper-empirics.md`](../types/paper-empirics.md).
- A cut plan keeps in the main text every finding the introduction relies on. It moves tables and figures to the appendix intact.

## Close

1. Before sign-off, the lead writes one coauthor note in the record: what changed in whose section; what needs their confirmation; each material issue with its source cell. The author sends it.
2. Build clean with the markup off.
3. Count the parts approved at first showing, after one correction, and sent back to the plan. Write the counts in `DECISIONS.md`.
