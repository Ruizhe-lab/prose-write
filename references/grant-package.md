# Grant package: build and submit the whole application

Who reads it: the coordinator, always; the lead, for a new application or a substantial revision.
The coordinator is the person or assistant who handles files, the budget, the tracker and the portal.
This file holds only what a whole application adds. Prose rules and checks: [types/grant.md](../types/grant.md).
Everything else is in the core. Roles, mode, who writes, versions, the message, approval, delivery, the record: [HOW_WE_WORK.md](../HOW_WE_WORK.md). The plan, the send check, the fresh reader, the fidelity read: [the unit file](unit-plan-card-gate.md).
The funder's current requirements and the author's current instructions govern.

Principle: the heavy work happens before the author reads. By the walk, the text should be nearly finished, so the walk is quick.

## Set up a new call (coordinator, the day the call is verified)

1. Verify the call on the funder's own page. Record the decision to prepare it, with the date the draft will reach the author.
2. Make an application folder with a plan file and a row in the grant tracker.
3. Fill the plan file: writer, write scope, next stop, reviewer per deliverable. The lead confirms its own line.
4. Set deadline reminders, for example at 7, 3, 1 and 0 days before the deadline, only for calls the author has said to pursue.

- A call reaches the lead first: the lead passes the call, the deadline and the author's instructions to the coordinator the same day.
- No funder deadline: use the agreed planning date.
- The coordinator does not answer after one retry: say so in the fit message. List the package duties left undone as open items.

## Fit (lead, first day, after recovering past grants and requirements)

The fit message is one prepared message. It holds:
1. The funder, its criteria, the deadline and the date the author will have the text.
2. Our fit.
3. Two or three arcs drawn from the author's past grants.
4. A recommendation and its real tradeoff.
5. A separate block: the facts only the author can give (below).

- When the section plans follow it: [types/grant.md](../types/grant.md), "Different from the general case".
- No answer: draft on the recommended arc, as the unit file says for a choice that is the author's. Send one reminder on the "fit agreed" date, as a slip warning ("Schedule" below).

## Facts only the author can give

- Salary lines, partner commitments, references, consent. The coordinator lists them. The lead asks once, with a recommendation, in the fit message.
- Salary: use the author's standing default if they have one. Otherwise ask.
- No default for references, consent or a partner's commitment. Write a marked gap, e.g. "[partner cost share: not yet confirmed]". Never fill a gap with a plausible number.
- Reference requests: the coordinator prepares the emails as drafts for the author and records a latest-ask date in the tracker.
- The author's answers go in the plan file.

## Requirements and required documents (coordinator)

List every required document at the start, each with an owner in the plan file and a milestone in the tracker.

| Kind of document | Owner |
|---|---|
| Factual (CV, current and pending support, standard facilities) | Coordinator, from records. The author sees them in the final review. |
| Restates the approved proposal (cover letter, data plan) | Lead. It is a short text: [types/grant.md](../types/grant.md), "Workflow, step by step". |
| Scored by the call on its own argument | Lead and partner, as a section (the team file, "Who writes"). |
| Letters of support | Outside people write them. Ask on day one. |

## Budget

- The coordinator owns the budget file and its numbers.
- Quote the funder's caps and allowable costs at its top.
- Name the source of every rate (fringe, indirect, tuition), or write "to be confirmed by research administration".
- Fund the actual people, compute and data. Never copy another grant's amounts.
- Budget prose the author reads is the use-of-funds role. The lead writes it from the budget file, never from the call alone.
- No budget file yet: the lead asks for it at once and works on other units.
- Reconcile every changed allocation across proposal, budget and form.
- Do not flag overlap with other proposals that are still undecided. Overlap matters only once an award exists.

## Figures and page

- Who designs and who renders: [types/grant.md](../types/grant.md), "Workflow, step by step". Name the figure's owner in the plan file.
- Reserve figure space in the page budget. Never fit the page by shrinking type.
- Format: the call's template; else LaTeX to PDF; Word only if required.

## Portal (coordinator)

- Save a draft and stop. Submitting is the author's act.
- A saved draft, uploaded files and a submission are three different states.
- Read each saved field back from the portal. Keep a reviewed screenshot in the application folder.
- A pasted portal summary is reported in one line: "Portal summary = your approved abstract, word for word (N words, limit M)".

## Schedule

- Milestones: fit agreed, plans agreed, sections ready, whole ready. The lead sets them. The coordinator records them in the tracker.
- Each milestone date is the day by which the author has agreed, not the day a message is sent. The fit message goes out on day 1. For a ten-day call: fit agreed by day 3, plans by day 4 or 5.
- Prepared mode: "plans agreed" is met when the plans are sent. Do not chase the author for it. Only the fit gets a reminder.
- "Ready" means reconciled, rendered and delivered to the author. It is due 3 days before the deadline.
- A slip: warn the author at once through the agreed channel. Say which milestone slipped, the new date, and what will be cut.

## Readiness test (lead, before "ready")

This is the fresh read of a prepared whole proposal. It replaces the general questions of the unit file.

- The fresh reader gets only the rendered PDF and the call's description of its reviewers.
- It says, in its own words:
  1. what the project is for;
  2. what the team has shown;
  3. what the award will do;
  4. how the methods answer it;
  5. why the funder could care;
  6. what it remembers from headings, bold and figures alone.
- Pass: each answer matches the intended belief; nothing material is missing or wrong.
- Also compare the candidate prose and rendered pages with the author's accepted grant. Carry forward its hierarchy, paragraph construction, selective emphasis and explanatory figures, adapted to this call. A plain default rendering is not evidence that the style was applied. Record and resolve material differences.
- Do not run a reviewer-style critique on the author's own proposal unless they ask.

## Ready notice and approval

- The ready notice: [HOW_WE_WORK.md](../HOW_WE_WORK.md), "Prepared work". A grant adds the funder's deadline, and each open decision with its evidence and recommendation.
- The walk that follows: [HOW_WE_WORK.md](../HOW_WE_WORK.md). Keep backstage checks off the walk.
- The question for a whole proposal, never for one walked section: "Do you approve this PDF as the proposal?" A yes covers only the displayed scope ([HOW_WE_WORK.md](../HOW_WE_WORK.md), approval).
- A text outside the PDF (a portal answer, a cover letter) is shown on its own, and again in the final review of stored answers.

## Two end states

- Writing done: every part is delivered ([HOW_WE_WORK.md](../HOW_WE_WORK.md)).
- Package done: submitted by the author, confirmation saved in the application folder, tracker updated. Until then the tracker says "revised proposal ready".
- Record a deliberate non-submission with its next action.
