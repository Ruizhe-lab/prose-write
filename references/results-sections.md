# Results: question, exact claim and explanation

A results section should let a reader explain what is being solved, what follows, why it follows and what changes because of it. Correct sentences and a complete proof inventory are not enough. A headline and a full characterization have different jobs.

## Choose the argument before the wording

Recover the adopted scientific claims, unresolved source notes, current author decisions and immediately preceding prose. Choose the section's distinct question and desired supported understanding. Then separate the main finding, its essential teaching prerequisites, other findings with independent reader jobs, and technical support.

Choose the statement's content **before** inventorying prerequisites. Certification establishes what is true, not what every main-text statement must contain. An optional height, auxiliary bound or helper identity may manufacture notation that the actual question does not need. Conversely, an uncited finding can still be economically central. Judge by reader job, not citation count.

Specify comparisons: what varies, what is held fixed, who adjusts, which equilibrium class applies, and how outcomes at different parameters are matched. Selection is not a tie-break inside an unchanged optimization unless the model says so; a new commitment stage is another institution. Do not silently turn selected uniqueness into base-game uniqueness.

Build a chain of questions. Do not interrupt one result's explanation with another calculation solely because its proof came next. When merging sections, rebuild the argument instead of concatenating their previews and conclusions. Give a switch, jump or domain change a home wherever omission would create a false inference; do not repeat every technical qualification everywhere.

## Open with the needed situation

The opening supplies the current question, relevant prior result and indispensable object or comparison. It need not preview all conclusions, explain all forces or repeat the roadmap.

Possible openings include a question, a short recall of established behavior followed by the new consequence to examine, or the definition of a benchmark that the result actually compares with. Choose the function from the reader's current knowledge, not from a stock phrase.

Before writing a pre-result paragraph, apply the **add-nothing test**: read the preceding prose. If it already states the question, object and comparison, begin the result. Otherwise supply the missing orientation. The forces explaining the answer usually belong after the statement unless the statement cannot be understood without them. A brief teaching calculation can appropriately precede it when that calculation makes the object intelligible.

## Write the formal statement

State premises and domain, then the complete conclusions promised. Use familiar actor and object names. Conditions must govern all dependent clauses; moving a high-domain qualifier into a separate sentence can accidentally universalize the next sentence.

Group conclusions by a real economic relationship: actor, common domain, mechanism or object. Do not preserve an old item count after its content changes. A common sufficient domain may be clearer than a list of maximal domains when the question concerns simultaneous effects.

A full equilibrium result names every endogenous strategy component and entry/clearing condition, including relevant corners. Recall earlier play by its action and exact reference. A policy threshold alone is not the full profile.

Keep necessary quantifiers and existence restrictions. Do not overload the statement with prose defending its robustness when the conclusion is already unconditional; a material interpretation can be explained afterward. “There is a threshold” and “the smallest threshold is” are different claims. An optimum's location and its value are different outputs.

An explicit maintained scope can avoid repeated assumption inventories. A source comment cannot replace a hypothesis the reader needs. Do not use the results section to re-explain the setup's assumptions, list every excluded strategy or rank alternatives unused by the result, unless that is itself a central finding.

## Explain the result in the right causal order

Select the reader's next question. Explain the supported change through the relevant choice, constraint or comparison. Do not simply restate the proposition in prose or follow the order of algebraic manipulations.

Check the actual attribution. A positive sum can have one term that drives the sign; do not say another term is sufficient without evidence. An accounting equality may be valuable, but it does not automatically explain an incentive. Expected identities must not be read as realized payment rules.

Name actors when a paragraph begins or its subject changes. Use pronouns once the antecedent is immediate and unique. Constantly repeating full actor names can also burden the paragraph.

Distinguish exposure from realized loss, quantity from return, gross from net income, total from per-unit effects and event duration from time shares. A passive quantity adjustment can coexist with unchanged net returns. Do not imply a transfer merely because one group's income rises while another group's quantity falls.

Keep concise explanations connected. If more than one benefit matters, retain the trade-off they answer. A reduction in one waiting interval does not necessarily reduce an entire cycle. A ratio comparison requires proportional changes, not only directions.

## Mathematics and technical homes

Retain short source-checked derivations that teach an important economic force. Move routine verification and unused helper objects to the proof. A display earns space by clarifying a definition, comparison, result or essential calculation, not by being available.

The appendix must retain required support and promised claims. Before removal or movement, check substantive information, labels, definitions, proof pointers, empirical predictions and captions. A clean main statement does not authorize deleting its assumptions or unsupported downstream references.

## Compare and review complete units

Read actual published examples performing the same result function. Compare the pre-result orientation, formal statement and discussion together. A component construction inside a larger equilibrium calls for a different exemplar than a full-game existence theorem. Do not borrow a familiar game's assumptions from an exemplar.

Review source truth and task fit separately. The fresh reader should recover the domain, object and economic implication from actual context. Reconcile the complete unit before displaying it. Preserve accepted material when it still serves the job; authorized reconstruction includes reconsidering previously approved passages, with their history retained.

At section completion, inspect the whole argument, not only the latest proposition: opening, hierarchy, transitions, figures, empirical bridge and conclusion. Stop when it meets its purpose and no material finding remains unresolved, rather than after an arbitrary number of review rounds.
